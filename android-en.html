1.2.0 has a new signing key. Uninstall the old version before upgrading

1.1.0 and earlier were signed with a public debug key, so anyone could make a look-alike package. From 1.2.0 on, EasyHub uses its own key. After uninstalling you need to sign in to GitHub again; your projects on GitHub are not affected.

Features

Keep up with your projects on the go

The app reads straight from GitHub. Publish an update on your computer and it shows up when you open the project on your phone.

Your home page is your to-do list

Needs your attention puts a project's open issues and pull requests to review on one row and folds away the rest, so you know what to do first.

Reply to issues right away

Open, reply to, close or reopen issues, and keep talking with the people who use your project.

See pull requests clearly

See which files changed. Before you approve and merge, you see the number of changed files and the check results, with a warning if checks failed.

Projects and releases in your pocket

Read READMEs, history and release notes, then download files straight to your phone with progress and a cancel button.

Discover projects

Search public projects and people, and browse EasyHub Trending. Translate READMEs and issues with one tap.

Dark mode

Choose Follow system, Light or Dark under Settings → Appearance. The colors match the desktop app and are easy on the eyes at night.

Phone and desktop

Check on your phone, publish on your computer

Sign in with the same GitHub account on both. Projects, issues and releases stay in sync through GitHub.

On your phone

  • Browse your projects and public projects
  • Open and reply to issues
  • Review, merge or close pull requests
  • Create projects and download releases

Needs the desktop app

  • Manage project folders on your computer
  • Publish code
  • Publish or edit releases
